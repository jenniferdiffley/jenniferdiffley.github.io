About

Part boundary pusher, part psychologist.

I’m a UX writer, senior copywriter, and digital content manager rolled into one — with a dump truck full of “I’ll learn that so I know it.”

At Amazon I’ve been the principal writer on products that had no vocabulary yet: a self-service supply-side advertising platform, a branded AI shopping assistant, and sponsored local services. That work is less about polishing microcopy and more about deciding what things are called, what order they’re understood in, and what a customer is entitled to know before they commit.

Before UX writing, I spent a decade in brand and campaign copy — autonomous vehicles, cybersecurity, enterprise SaaS, beauty. It taught me how to make highly technical subject matter into something someone actually wants to read.

  • UX writing — wireframes to A/B testing
  • Foundational documents — content style guides to workflow processes
  • Information architecture — taxonomy, naming systems, content hierarchy
  • Conversation design — AI personality, prompts, dialogue flows
  • Cross-functional partnership — design, PM, engineering, applied science, legal
  • Performance metrics — Google Analytics to in-platform data
